## Fine-tuned Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct — `qwen-task-extractor-q4_k_m.gguf`

This model is a **modified version** of Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct by the Qwen
team, Alibaba Cloud (Copyright 2024 Alibaba Cloud), licensed under the
Apache License, Version 2.0. A copy of that licence is in
[`Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-LICENSE.txt`](Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-LICENSE.txt).

Modifications, made by Gaurav Verma in September 2026:

- Supervised fine-tuning with LoRA adapters (Unsloth), starting from
  `unsloth/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct`, on a synthetic dataset generated by
  `scripts/dataset/generate_sft.py` (to-do extraction and note-grounded
  answers). Training notebook: `notebooks/qwen_sft_unsloth.ipynb`.
- The adapters were merged into the base weights and the result was
  converted to GGUF and quantized to Q4_K_M.
